#### Khmis-Sanad
# 🌿 Marketplace Artisanale Multi-Vendeurs

> An e-commerce platform bridging consumers directly with local artisans and cooperatives. Built with a decoupled architecture featuring a **Laravel 10+ REST API** and a **React 18+ (Vite) Single Page Application**[cite: 2].

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Local cooperatives and artisans in Morocco often struggle to showcase their products on a large scale due to multiple intermediaries and a lack of tailored digital tools[cite: 2]. This platform provides:
* **Direct Artisan-to-Buyer Connection:** Instant communication via a direct WhatsApp messaging channel for order confirmation[cite: 2].
* **Autonomous Vendor Management:** Dedicated storefronts for cooperatives to manage products, pricing, discounts, and inventory directly from a mobile-first dashboard[cite: 2].
* **Storytelling & Traceability:** Focus on highlighting the terroir, artisan background, and product origins[cite: 2].
* **Fluid User Experience:** A fast Single Page Application (SPA) designed for mobile-first navigation[cite: 2].

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛍️ Buyers (Clients)
* **Catalog & Filtering:** Filter products by category (oils, spices, honey, etc.) to quickly locate items[cite: 2].
* **Product Details & Storytelling:** Detailed product pages displaying price, stock levels, and the producer's story[cite: 2].
* **Asynchronous Cart:** Add items to cart asynchronously without page interruptions[cite: 2].
* **WhatsApp Order Validation:** Finalize orders and start direct discussions with artisans over WhatsApp[cite: 2].

### 🏺 Artisans / Cooperatives
* **Storefront Personalization:** Customize boutique names, biographies, and terroir origins[cite: 2].
* **Inventory Management:** Add, update, and manage products, stock levels, prices, and discounts via an intuitive dashboard[cite: 2].
* **Email Alerts:** Receive automated email notifications whenever a new order is validated[cite: 2].

### 🛡️ Administration & Moderation
* **Artisan Onboarding:** Approve or suspend cooperative accounts to ensure catalog quality standards[cite: 2].
* **Community Moderation:** Moderate user comments and reviews to maintain a healthy platform environment[cite: 2].
